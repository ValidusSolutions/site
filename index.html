Healthcare · Assessment

An assessment that became the roadmap

Brought in by a national healthcare regulatory organization to plan how to merge two APIs and standardize authentication. The assessment also found more than a dozen NuGet packages with critical vulnerabilities, another dozen several versions behind, and a Node.js version several major releases out of date.

The plan put security and stability first, then the requested work, with an estimate, the skill sets needed and a process for keeping dependencies current.

Two weeks of close collaboration led to a multi-month engagement to secure and stabilize the system.
